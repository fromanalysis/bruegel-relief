shell

Both walls attenuate identically, so both transmit the same picture. In the hand the outward shell is crisp and the inward one is a grey ghost below 2 mm: an outward shell in a lit room runs two registered channels, transmission for the tone and room light on the relief for the drawing.

How fast the filament absorbs. In the hand: too dark, raise it; too milky, lower it. The 1.25 default is a placeholder — nobody has printed the calibration wedge yet.

wrap and resolution

The mirror follows the picture with its own reflection, so both joins are mirror lines and neither is a pictorial edit. It doubles the diameter.

0.25 mm is oversampled against a 0.42 mm bead. Coarser makes a smaller STL and a faster export, and nothing else.

view

The preview is decimated so it can be rebuilt while a slider moves, which makes it read smoother than the object is — at every 4th sample the circumferential slope measures about half what the exported mesh carries. Drag right to see the real thing; at the far right the preview is the exported mesh, which on a large painting is two million faces and will be slow. Nothing else changes: the readouts and the STL are full resolution at every setting.

Beer–Lambert through the wall. This is the picture the lamp makes, and it is not the heightfield: the map is exponential, so the thin end spreads and the thick end crushes.

printing it

0.12 mm layers, 100 % infill, Concentric, wall loops to maximum, white PLA, brim, no supports. Vase mode is impossible — the wall thickness is the picture. Do not scale the STL in the slicer: scaling changes the wall, which is the tone map.

On the outward shell, paint the seam. The picture's own hard edges are sharper corners than any groove the wall can afford, so the seam channel loses to them on almost every layer.

about this build

A JavaScript port of the lamp path of painting2model, verified field by field against cylinder.py: worst deviation anywhere on the object 1.1e-5 mm, which is 3e-5 of a bead. The reference's own sort ambiguity moves it 170 times further than the port does.

Everything runs in this page. No upload, no server, no network.

It is the lamp path of a longer write-up: the build guide takes a painting to a flat panel, and From Panel to Cylinder is where this shell, its seam and the printed pair that settled which way the relief faces are worked out.

painting2ball is the same idea on a sphere: a hanging ornament instead of a lamp, and a harder mapping — no rectangle meets a sphere without distortion, so the projection becomes a choice you make.
